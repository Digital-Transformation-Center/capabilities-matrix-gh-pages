# Airframe Arm Deflection & Strain Test Bench

- **Physical:** A custom mechanical test jig constructed using aluminum T-slot framing and 3D-printed mounts, holding a 3D-printed or carbon fiber UAV arm under static and dynamic motor thrust loads.
        
- **Digital/Metrology:** Utilizes high-resolution stereoscopic cameras (DIC) to track optical surface deformation in real-time, comparing empirical deflection data against Ansys FEA predictions.
    
- **Member Interest:** Optical metrology (Trilion), simulation validation (Ansys), structural materials testing (UDRI).
