# Modular Quick-Change Cobot Maintenance Tooling

- **Physical:** A benchtop cobot arm fitted with custom 3D-printed end-effectors (grippers, inspection probes, or fastener drivers) using magnetic or pneumatic tool changers.
    
- **Digital/AI:** Vision-guided positioning for automated inspection or component replacement tasks.
    
- **Member Interest:** Automated sustainment and repair (AFRL RSO), smart manufacturing workforce training (Sinclair, NPower).
