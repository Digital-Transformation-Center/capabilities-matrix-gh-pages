# Automated Twinning of Paint Application for Quality Assurance

- **Physical:** A robotic arm equipped with a custom end effector that includes a paint thickness gauge and high-resolution camera for surface inspection.

- **Digital/AI:** Utilizes computer vision and machine learning algorithms to analyze paint thickness measurements and detect surface defects, providing real-time feedback for process optimization.

- **Member Interest:** Machine learning for defect detection and multiphysics co-simulation (Ansys), 3D optical metrology and physical-to-digital validation (Trilion Quality Systems), embedded ML and algorithm execution (MathWorks), materials and human factors (UDRI).
