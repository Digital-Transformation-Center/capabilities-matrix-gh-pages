# ML-Informed Material Stress Visualization

- **Physical:** Composite material samples instrumented with sensors to measure stress and strain fields.
    
- **Digital/AI:** Utilizes microscale neural operators to predict and project stress and strain fields onto the sample surface for real-time visualization.
    
- **Member Interest:** Ceramic Matrix Composites (CMCs) testing and physics-based failure modeling (UDRI), 3D optical strain tracking and Digital Image Correlation (Trilion Quality Systems), real-time multiphysics co-simulation and visualization (Ansys), immersive AR/VR digital twin projections (Bloc Digital).
