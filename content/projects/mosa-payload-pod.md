# MOSA-Compliant Smart Payload Pod

- **Physical:** A 3D-printed, quick-release modular payload enclosure housing custom PCBs (power distribution, sensor signal conditioning) that connects via a standardized physical and electrical pinout interface.
    
- **Digital/AI:** Automatically broadcasts its payload descriptor and telemetry schema upon connection to an onboard edge computer.
    
- **Member Interest:** MOSA hardware integration (TangramFlex, Psoas), avionics retrofits (Field Aerospace, Sumaria), custom fabrication (Sinclair, ARCTOS).
