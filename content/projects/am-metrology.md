# Closed-Loop Additive Manufacturing In-Situ Metrology Rig

- **Physical:** A desktop 3D printer instrumented with a thermal camera and optical microscope aimed at the print nozzle.
    
- **Digital/AI:** Detects layer misalignments or thermal void formations during the build and feeds layer-by-layer defect maps back into CAD/FEA software to predict part strength loss before completion.
    
- **Member Interest:** Smart manufacturing (ARCTOS, Sinclair), digital thread integration (AFMC DTO, UDRI), physics simulation (Ansys).
