# Rigging & Construction

Provides the framework for portable and adaptable physical prototypes. The Rigging & Construction capability allows users to quickly assemble mounting platforms for a wide variety of applications. 

## Key Focus Areas
- Aluminum T-Slot fabrication (cutting, drilling).
- Readily-available assembly components (screws, nuts, brackets).
- Carbon fiber construction.
