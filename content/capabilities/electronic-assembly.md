# Electronic Assembly

Assemble and test electronic components and circuits for prototyping and production.

## Focus Areas

- Soldering and desoldering of components on PCBs.
- Testing and debugging of electronic circuits.
- Creating custom wiring harnesses and connectors for integration into larger systems.
