# Metrology & Inspection

The Metrology & Inspection capability provides users with precise optical and digital measurement equipment to rapidly inspect fabricated parts, verify physical dimensions, and track surface deformation against CAD and simulation models.

## Focus Areas

- Non-contact 3D surface deformation and optical strain tracking (DIC).
- Precision dimensional measurement and quality inspection of fabricated parts.
- Physical validation of computational simulation models (FEA/CFD).
