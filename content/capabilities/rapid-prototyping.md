# Rapid Prototyping

The Rapid Prototyping capability allows users to quickly create physical components for projects with short timelines or rapid iteration cycles. This capability is ideal for testing and validating designs before committing to full-scale production.

## Focus Areas

- 3D printing for rapid iteration of parts and assemblies.
- CNC machining for precision components.
- Laser cutting for quick fabrication of flat materials.
