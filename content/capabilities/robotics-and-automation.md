# Robotics & Automation

The Robotics & Automation capability provides users with compact collaborative robots, motion controllers, and vision guidance sensors to rapidly prototype automated manipulation, assembly, inspection, and maintenance sequences.

## Focus Areas

- Collaborative robot (cobot) programming and end-effector integration.
- Vision-guided positioning and target tracking for automated inspection and maintenance.
- Quick-change tool interfaces (magnetic, pneumatic, mechanical) for multi-purpose robotic tasks.
