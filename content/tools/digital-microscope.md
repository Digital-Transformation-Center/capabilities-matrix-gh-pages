# Digital Inspection Microscope

High-magnification digital optical microscope used in the DTC Model Shop for non-destructive visual inspection, fine-pitch PCB solder joint verification, and real-time process monitoring.

## Features
- Continuous optical zoom with high-resolution HD camera feed for live display.
- Dimmable LED ring illumination for glare-free surface and feature inspection.
- Real-time video output and image capture for micro-defect documentation and layer analysis.
- Adjustable boom stand adaptable to desktop 3D printers, PCB workbenches, and inspection fixtures.
