# High-Resolution Thermal Imaging Camera

Precision radiometric infrared camera used in the DTC Model Shop for real-time thermal analysis of active electronics, high-power edge compute modules, and mechanical components under load.

## Features
- High thermal sensitivity and radiometric temperature mapping for identifying localized hot spots.
- Non-contact thermal imaging ideal for evaluating heatsinks, cooling fan shrouds, and enclosure airflow.
- Video recording and thermal data logging for transient thermal dissipation analysis.
- Color palette overlays and radiometric image export for thermal simulation validation.
