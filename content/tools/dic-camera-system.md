# Digital Image Correlation (DIC) Optical System

Stereoscopic optical metrology tool used in the DTC Model Shop to capture high-speed 3D surface deformation, strain distribution, and displacement fields without physical contact.

## Features
- High-resolution dual stereo camera setup with synchronized high-speed capture.
- Real-time full-field 3D strain mapping and displacement tracking under load.
- Direct export of optical deformation mesh data for comparison against FEA simulation models.
- Non-contact measurement ideal for lightweight composite, carbon fiber, and 3D-printed components.
