# Soldering & Rework Station

Precision electronic assembly and rework station used in the DTC Model Shop for wire harness fabrication, through-hole PCB assembly, surface-mount (SMD) soldering, and circuit component desoldering/repair.

## Features
- Dual-function temperature-controlled soldering iron and hot air rework tool.
- Precision tips for fine-pitch surface-mount (SMD) components and dense PCB assemblies.
- High-capacity iron for heavy gauge wiring and wire harness soldering.
- Integrated vacuum desoldering pump and wick for rapid component removal and board repair.
- Variable temperature and airflow controls suitable for leaded and lead-free alloys.
- ESD-safe design to protect sensitive microelectronics and ICs during assembly.
