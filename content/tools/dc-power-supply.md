# Programmable DC Bench Power Supply & Analyzer

Benchtop multi-channel programmable DC power supply used in the DTC Model Shop to deliver regulated voltage rails and measure dynamic power consumption across electronic prototypes and edge compute hardware.

## Features
- Multi-channel isolated DC outputs with precise voltage and current limit regulation.
- High-resolution real-time voltage, current, and wattage monitoring.
- Dynamic data logging for profiling power spikes, idle power, and watt-per-inference metrics.
- Over-voltage and over-current protection (OVP/OCP) to safeguard sensitive development boards.
