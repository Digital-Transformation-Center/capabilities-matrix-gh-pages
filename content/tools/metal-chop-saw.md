# Industrial Metal Chop Saw

Heavy-duty physical shop tool used in the DTC Model Shop for preparing structural aluminum, struts, steel or carbon fiber tubing, and custom rigging brackets.

### Features
- 15 Amp 5.5 HP motor
- 14-inch abrasive wheel
- Quick-lock vise for fast material clamping
