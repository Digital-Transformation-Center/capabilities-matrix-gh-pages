# Oscilloscope

High-precision electronic test equipment used in the DTC Model Shop for visualizing voltage signals, troubleshooting PCB circuits, and analyzing signal integrity.

### Features
- Multi-channel digital storage oscilloscope for real-time signal capture.
- High sampling rate and bandwidth for signal integrity and timing analysis.
- Hardware serial bus decoding (I2C, SPI, UART, CAN) for digital communication debugging.
- Automated measurement and waveform math capabilities.
